Colour Do's & Don'ts
✓ Do
  • Use #0D1F4E as the primary button and header colour
  • Use white backgrounds with blue accents for cards
  • Use #0D1F4E only for footer and hover states
  • Use #04B4FD for dividers, badges, and secondary elements
  • Maintain the 60/25/10/4/1 ratio per screen
✗ Don't
  • Don't use colours outside this palette (e.g. random greens or reds for branding)
  • Don't place dark blue (#0D1F4E) as a main background for entire pages
  • Don't use low-contrast combos like light blue text on white
  • Don't overuse the accent dark blue — it is meant to be rare
  • Don't use colour alone to convey status — always pair with text or icons

✓ Do
  • Use Poppins for all headings (H1–H3) and button labels
  • Use Open Sans for body copy, descriptions, and meta text
  • Keep H1 at 40px — do not make page titles smaller
  • Use #0D1F4E for H3 card titles to create visual hierarchy
  • Keep body text at minimum 16px for accessibility
✗ Don't
  • Don't use more than 2 font families on any page
  • Don't use font weights lighter than 400 (Regular)
  • Don't set body text below 14px — it becomes unreadable
  • Don't use ALL CAPS for body text (only acceptable in small labels)
  • Don't mix Poppins and Open Sans in the same sentence

Section 4
Logo Usage
The UniTrade logo must always have clear space around it and may only appear in approved colour versions.
ContextMinimum WidthNotes
Desktop / Tablet website120pxWordmark + icon mark
Mobile website / app header90pxIcon + abbreviated wordmark if needed
Print (business cards, flyers)25mm (~1 inch)Use vector format only
Favicon / App icon32 × 32pxIcon only — no wordmark
✓ Do
  • Use the logo exactly as provided — no modifications
  • Always maintain clear space (no text or graphics crowding the logo)
  • Use the reversed white version on dark backgrounds
  • Scale the logo proportionally when resizing
  • Use the icon alone as a favicon or app badge
✗ Don't
  • Don't stretch or distort the logo disproportionately
  • Don't change logo colours except to approved mono versions (white or black)
  • Don't place the logo on busy photography without a solid backing or scrim
  • Don't rotate or tilt the logo
  • Don't add drop shadows, glows, or outlines to the logo
Section 5
Tone of Voice
UniTrade speaks like a trustworthy peer — not a corporation. Short sentences, active voice, honest language. Here are real examples of what that sounds like.
Write Like This

"We verify, you trade safely."

Not Like This

"We are not responsible for transactions conducted on this platform."

Write Like This

"Check in when you arrive."

Not Like This

"Location verification shall be performed upon arrival at the designated meetup point."

Write Like This

"No payment until you meet and inspect in person."

Not Like This

"Payment processing will be withheld until physical inspection has occurred."

Write Like This

"Your listing was rejected. Here's why and how to fix it."

Not Like This

"Your submission did not meet the required criteria and has been declined."

Three Core Principles
Trustworthy, not cold
We protect users without hiding behind legalese.
Student-first, not academic
Short sentences. Active voice. Real language.
Direct about rules
No fine print. No hidden catches.
Section 6
Imagery & Photography
Photography choices reflect the real South African student experience — not polished stock imagery.
✓ Use
  • Real students on South African campuses (UP, Wits, UCT, etc.)
  • Study sessions, meetups at libraries and cafes
  • Textbook close-ups and phone showing the app
  • Warm, natural lighting with no heavy filters
  • Overlays over hero images to ensure text contrast
✗ Avoid
  • Generic "businesspeople" stock photos
  • Foreign campuses that don't reflect SA student life
  • Heavy Instagram-style filters or colour grading
  • Posed, unnatural looking studio photos
  • Images that don't relate to academic or student life
